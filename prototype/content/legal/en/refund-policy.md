## 1. What a Balance payment buys

Genora Balance is prepaid access to a digital service. A request can incur an AI-provider cost after credit is added, so credit already spent on a completed request is normally not refundable as a change-of-mind request. This document covers duplicate charges, missing credit, unauthorised payment, technical billing errors and statutory consumer withdrawal. Mandatory consumer rights and remedies for faulty or misdescribed digital content or services always prevail.

## 2. Digital service and withdrawal rights

The customer may have a statutory withdrawal period under the law of the country where the contract is made. This policy does not waive that right. For a service, if a consumer expressly asks for performance to start during the withdrawal period, the law may require payment for the proportion of the service supplied before cancellation. For digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, a withdrawal exception may apply only after the required express consent to immediate supply and acknowledgment of the resulting loss of that right have actually been obtained. Genora relies on that exception only where those steps were obtained at checkout; account creation or general acceptance of the Terms alone does not remove statutory rights. Fault, description and reasonable-care remedies remain available regardless.

## 3. When Genora may correct or refund

After checking the account and payment record, Genora may approve a refund or first correct the Balance where any of these is confirmed:

1. one top-up was charged more than once;
2. a payment succeeded but the credit never appeared and cannot be fixed technically;
3. the payment was unauthorised;
4. a Genora billing defect charged twice or debited the wrong amount;
5. a confirmed Genora failure or outage directly prevented use of the newly purchased Balance, or another statutory remedy applies because the service was not provided as agreed or with reasonable care and skill;
6. a qualifying withdrawal concerns unused Balance;
7. Genora ended access for a reason unrelated to the customer’s breach; or
8. another refund is required by law.

## 4. Cases that normally do not qualify

No refund is normally due for:

- a disappointing result by itself, including an inaccurate, refused or unsuitable model result; this does not exclude a statutory remedy where digital content or a service is faulty, not as described or was not provided with reasonable care and skill;
- an incorrect prompt, source file, model choice or price choice;
- a changed mind or a later decision not to use the service;
- access obtained through unprotected credentials before compromise was reported;
- automation, loops, scripts or repeated requests initiated by the customer;
- an outage or model change caused by a provider while other Genora functionality remained available;
- termination caused by violation of the Terms, Acceptable Use Policy, sanctions rules or provider rules;
- bonus or promotional credit;
- a request too late to verify, unless a longer legal period applies; or
- a pattern showing refund abuse, multiple accounts or a chargeback after use.

## 5. How to submit a request

Email **support@genora.art** from the account address with subject “Refund”. Give the transaction ID, date, amount and currency, payment method, reason and supporting evidence. The recommended period is 14 calendar days from payment; later requests remain possible where the facts can be checked or the law gives more time.

For fraud protection Genora may ask for reasonable proof that the requester controls the account and payment instrument. An unexplained refusal can prevent approval.

## 6. Amount calculated

The refundable amount is the unused portion of paid Balance at the crediting tariff. To the extent legally allowed, Genora excludes consumed resources, bonus credit, non-refundable bank or payment-provider fees, non-refundable taxes and exchange-rate differences. Genora may offer an equivalent Balance correction, but cannot replace a monetary refund where mandatory law requires money back.

## 7. Timing and destination

Genora normally reviews a complete request within 10 business days. If a refund is required by law, this internal target does not extend the applicable legal deadline. Where UK consumer law applies, a qualifying refund is made without undue delay and normally within 14 days after the entitlement is agreed or otherwise established, to the original payment method and without a fee. An approved money refund goes to the original payment method. The payment provider or bank controls the final posting time, usually 3–20 business days. Crypto or another irreversible method may make a return in the original asset impossible; Genora will use a lawful equivalent remedy and will not use this limitation to remove a mandatory refund.

## 8. Chargebacks

If practical, contact support before opening a bank dispute so that a missing-credit or duplicate-charge problem can be resolved without delay. You may use any lawful card-dispute or chargeback right. While a chargeback is investigated, Genora may freeze only the disputed Balance and reverse credits connected to it to prevent double recovery. Genora will not penalise a good-faith exercise of a legal right; any action for fraud or repeated knowingly unfounded disputes is limited to what the law permits.

## 9. Account closure

Account deletion alone is not a refund request. Submit a refund request before deletion so that payment and Balance evidence remains available. If Genora terminates access without a customer breach, the Terms determine the treatment of unused paid Balance. Cancellation after 12 months of inactivity follows the Terms and does not create a refund except where law requires one.

## 10. Version applying to a payment

The version in force when payment was made governs that payment, subject to mandatory law. Material future changes are published with the notice required by the Terms of Use.
