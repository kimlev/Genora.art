## 1. The safety line

This policy sets the rules for Genora’s website, chat, agents, memory, files, automation and any API or administration feature. It forms part of the Terms of Use. It applies to an account holder, corporate administrator, invited user, contractor and anyone to whom the account holder gives access.

Provider rules also apply. If the selected AI provider has a stricter rule, follow the stricter rule. You remain responsible for the request, input and output even when an automatic route selected the model.

## 2. Lawful use

Do not use Genora to break a law, evade sanctions or export controls, infringe intellectual property, violate privacy or confidentiality, disclose a secret, breach a contract, or evade a block imposed by Genora, a provider or an authority. You must have the permissions and lawful basis needed for another person’s data, image, voice or work.

## 3. Content Genora will not permit

The following are zero-tolerance categories. Genora may reject the request, remove the material, suspend the account immediately and report it where a legal duty or permission exists:

1. harmful content generation in text, image, audio, video, code or any other form;
2. sexual material involving a minor, including description, sexualisation, stylisation or generation;
3. NSFW, pornography, erotica, explicit or sexually suggestive material, including adult sexual content and any such content in text, image, audio or video;
4. sexual exploitation, trafficking or forced labour;
5. terrorism, violent extremism or mass-violence assistance;
6. instructions for weapons, explosives, chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear agents;
7. malware, ransomware, exploits or tools intended to defeat security;
8. sexualised material of a real person without the required consent;
9. sexual violence;
10. extreme violence, gore or dismemberment;
11. zoophilia;
12. a deepfake of a person without that person’s consent; and
13. hate speech.

The prohibition covers requesting, uploading, transforming and generating the material. Genora is not an NSFW or harmful-content service.

The following require a lawful basis, appropriate controls and, where applicable, a licence; without those safeguards they are prohibited:

- deceptive deepfakes using a real person’s face, voice or signature;
- forged identity, government, payment or other official documents, seals or signatures;
- impersonation of a person, business, authority or Genora;
- defamation, harassment, bullying, threats, incitement or discrimination;
- fraud, phishing, social engineering, investment manipulation, KYC/AML evasion or get-rich-quick schemes;
- unsolicited bulk communication, spam, fake reviews, artificial engagement or invented identities;
- stalking, unlawful surveillance, de-anonymisation, facial recognition or profiling;
- regulated financial, medical or legal activity without the licence and controls that apply to it.

## 4. Protecting the service

Do not scan, penetrate, load-test or search for vulnerabilities without written permission; exploit a weakness; bypass login, quotas, filters, billing or safeguards; extract system prompts; reverse engineer or decompile; copy the catalogue or interface for a competing product; distribute malicious code; create excessive load; or use Genora as an open proxy or relay.

## 5. Model adversarial testing

Prompt injection, jailbreak attempts, filter bypass, training-data extraction and system-instruction extraction are Red Teaming. Written approval is required before any such work. Send a proposed scope, method, accounts, safeguards and reporting plan to support@genora.art. Unapproved testing may be blocked and recorded.

## 6. Automation and fair access

Use only the supplied interfaces and reasonable request rates. Do not spread traffic across accounts, keys, proxies or IP addresses to defeat a limit; create multiple accounts for bonuses or evasion; run uncontrolled retries; or resell access without written permission. Genora may alter quotas and parallelism to protect the service. You bear charges produced by your own automation or integration.

## 7. Sensitive data and high-impact decisions

Do not treat Genora as a repository for health or medical records, payment-card data subject to PCI DSS, biometric or genetic data, government identifiers, children’s data, state secrets or other specially protected information unless Genora has agreed additional safeguards in writing.

Do not use an output as the sole or decisive basis for medical care, lending, insurance, financial access, employment, education admission or assessment, public/social services, legal or migration status, sanctions, coercive measures, life safety or critical infrastructure. If your use is high-risk or regulated under AI law, you must provide the required risk assessment, human oversight, records, information and labelling. Manipulation exploiting vulnerabilities, social scoring and prohibited biometric categorisation are not allowed.

## 8. Corporate accounts

The corporate administrator controls invited users, retention, memory and logs, and must communicate this policy internally. The organisation is responsible for the actions of its users and customers who receive access to results.

## 9. Enforcement

Genora may use automated and human review where there is a reason to suspect abuse. Measures can include a warning, request limit, model or feature restriction, rejected request, content removal, suspension, termination, provider/payment notification, authority notification and a claim for losses. An immediate threat may be handled without prior notice. Unused Balance is not refunded after a breach to the extent permitted by law.

An appeal can be sent to support@genora.art; Genora may provide human review while retaining the final decision allowed by law. Genora is not required to preserve or return material removed for a violation.

## 10. Abuse reports

Send the time, description, relevant link or request identifier, your contact details and, for an IP complaint, evidence of authority and a good-faith statement to **support@genora.art**. Deliberately false reports intended to harm another user violate this policy.

## 11. Changes

Genora may revise this policy for new risks, provider rules or legislation. Material changes follow the notice procedure in the Terms. A change needed to stop an immediate threat can take effect on publication.
