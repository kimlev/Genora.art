## 5. Adversarial testing of models (Red Teaming)

5.1. **Red Teaming** — prompt injection, jailbreak, attempts to bypass a model’s protective mechanisms, extract training data or system instructions, and other adversarial impacts.

5.2. Such actions violate the rules of most AI Providers and are **prohibited without the Company’s prior written permission**.

5.3. A request for research testing is sent to support@genora.art with a description of the purpose, methodology, scope, protective measures, and the procedure for disclosing results. Permission is granted at the Company’s discretion, in individual cases and for a limited period.

5.4. Uncoordinated attempts are recorded by the monitoring systems of the Company and providers and generally result in termination of access, including on the provider’s side.
